Choose an app for your workflow

  • Whisper Transcriber Suite pairs Windows installer and portable builds with a batch queue, SRT and DOCX export, and a built-in downloader. On a Mac, pick the DMG for your chip and macOS version.
  • Subtitle Edit is designed for making and timing subtitles, with waveform editing and a choice of speech engines.
  • Vibe offers batch transcription and DOCX export across Windows, Mac and Linux. Buzz includes a watch folder and a command-line interface for scripting.
  • ScribeDrop has a drag-and-drop Windows queue with SRT and VTT output. noScribe has an editor geared to interviews and speaker turns.
  • MacWhisper has a free Mac tier with subtitle output. Its Pro tier adds batch transcription, speaker recognition and DOCX.
